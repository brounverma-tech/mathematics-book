# 📎 Appendix D: Viva Questions with Answers

## D.1 Algebra

1. **What is a matrix?**  
   A rectangular arrangement of elements in rows and columns.

2. **What is the order of a matrix?**  
   Number of rows × number of columns.

3. **When can two matrices be added?**  
   When they have the same order.

4. **Is matrix multiplication commutative?**  
   Generally no; $AB\ne BA$.

5. **What is a square matrix?**  
   A matrix with equal numbers of rows and columns.

6. **What is the determinant of a singular matrix?**  
   Zero.

7. **What is a minor?**  
   A determinant obtained after deleting one row and one column.

8. **What is the adjoint?**  
   The transpose of the cofactor matrix.

9. **When does an inverse exist?**  
   When the square matrix has non-zero determinant.

10. **What is the matrix form of a linear system?**  
    $AX=B$.

## D.2 Differential Calculus

11. **What does a limit describe?**  
    The value approached by a function near a point.

12. **When does a two-sided limit exist?**  
    When LHL and RHL exist and are equal.

13. **State the continuity condition.**  
    $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=f(a)$, with both sides defined.

14. **What is a derivative?**  
    Instantaneous rate of change or tangent slope.

15. **What is the derivative of $x^n$?**  
    $nx^{n-1}$.

16. **What does the chain rule handle?**  
    Composite functions.

17. **Why is $dy/dx$ attached in implicit differentiation?**  
    Because $y$ is itself a function of $x$.

18. **What is a parametric derivative?**  
    $(dy/dt)/(dx/dt)$.

19. **What extra condition does Rolle's theorem need?**  
    $f(a)=f(b)$.

20. **What does LMVT compare?**  
    Instantaneous tangent slope with average chord slope.

## D.3 Integral Calculus

21. **What is integration?**  
    The inverse process of differentiation.

22. **Why is $+C$ added?**  
    Different constants have the same derivative.

23. **What is an integrand?**  
    The function being integrated.

24. **When is substitution useful?**  
    When an inner expression and its derivative are present.

25. **State integration by parts.**  
    $\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du$.

26. **What does LIATE mean?**  
    Logarithmic, Inverse trig, Algebraic, Trigonometric, Exponential.

27. **When are partial fractions used?**  
    To integrate proper rational functions after factorization.

28. **Does a definite integral need $+C$?**  
    No.

29. **What is the integral of an odd function on $[-a,a]$?**  
    Zero.

30. **What is a Riemann sum?**  
    A limit of sums of function values times small interval widths.

## D.4 Complex Theory

31. **What is $i^2$?**  
    $-1$.

32. **What is the real part of $3-2i$?**  
    3.

33. **What is its imaginary part?**  
    $-2$.

34. **What is a conjugate?**  
    A complex number with the imaginary sign reversed.

35. **What is modulus?**  
    Distance of the complex point from the origin.

36. **What is Euler's formula?**  
    $e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$.

37. **What does De Moivre's theorem calculate?**  
    Integer powers of complex numbers in polar form.

38. **How many distinct nth roots does a non-zero complex number have?**  
    Exactly $n$.

39. **Where do roots of unity lie?**  
    On the unit circle.

40. **What is $1+\omega+\omega^2$ for cube roots of unity?**  
    Zero.
