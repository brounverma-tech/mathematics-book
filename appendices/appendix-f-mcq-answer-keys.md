# 📎 Appendix F: MCQ Practice Sets and Answer Keys

## F.1 Unit 1 — Algebra

1. The order of a matrix with 2 rows and 5 columns is:  
   A. $5\times2$  B. $2\times5$  C. 7  D. 10

2. Matrix addition requires:  
   A. Equal determinants  B. Same order  C. Square matrices  D. Non-zero entries

3. If $A$ is $2\times3$ and $B$ is $3\times4$, $AB$ is:  
   A. $3\times3$  B. $2\times4$  C. $4\times2$  D. Undefined

4. A determinant exists for:  
   A. Row matrix  B. Column matrix  C. Square matrix  D. Every matrix

5. Cofactor $C_{12}$ has sign:  
   A. Positive  B. Negative  C. Zero  D. Undefined

6. If $|A|=0$, $A$ is:  
   A. Identity  B. Singular  C. Scalar  D. Symmetric

7. Adjoint is:  
   A. Transpose of cofactor matrix  B. Determinant  C. Inverse  D. Rank

8. $(AB)^{-1}$ equals:  
   A. $A^{-1}B^{-1}$  B. $B^{-1}A^{-1}$  C. $AB$  D. $BA$

9. Cramer's rule requires:  
   A. $\Delta=0$  B. $\Delta\ne0$  C. Rectangular matrix  D. Zero matrix

10. Unequal coefficient and augmented ranks mean:  
    A. Unique solution  B. Infinite solutions  C. No solution  D. Two solutions

**Answer Key:** 1-B, 2-B, 3-B, 4-C, 5-B, 6-B, 7-A, 8-B, 9-B, 10-C

## F.2 Unit 2 — Differential Calculus

1. $\lim_{x\to0}\sin x/x$ is: A. 0  B. 1  C. $\infty$  D. Undefined
2. A two-sided limit needs: A. LHL > RHL  B. LHL = RHL  C. $f(a)=0$  D. None
3. Derivative of $x^4$ is: A. $x^3$  B. $4x^3$  C. $4x^4$  D. $x^5/5$
4. Derivative of $\cos x$ is: A. $\sin x$  B. $-\sin x$  C. $\cos x$  D. $-\cos x$
5. Derivative of $e^x$ is: A. 1  B. $xe^{x-1}$  C. $e^x$  D. $\ln x$
6. Chain rule applies to: A. Constant  B. Composite function  C. Matrix  D. Integral only
7. $d(y^2)/dx$ is: A. $2y$  B. $2yy'$  C. $y'$  D. $y^2$
8. Parametric $dy/dx$ is: A. $(dx/dt)/(dy/dt)$  B. $(dy/dt)/(dx/dt)$  C. $dx/dy$  D. $dt/dx$
9. Rolle requires: A. $f(a)\ne f(b)$  B. $f(a)=f(b)$  C. $f'(a)=0$  D. $a=b$
10. LMVT slope is: A. $[f(b)-f(a)]/(b-a)$  B. $f(a)+f(b)$  C. 0  D. $b-a$

**Answer Key:** 1-B, 2-B, 3-B, 4-B, 5-C, 6-B, 7-B, 8-B, 9-B, 10-A

4. Substitution reverses: A. Product rule  B. Chain rule  C. Quotient rule  D. Transpose
5. By-parts formula contains: A. Plus integral  B. Minus integral  C. No integral  D. Limit

7. Partial fractions require a directly decomposed fraction to be: A. Improper  B. Proper  C. Irrational  D. Constant
